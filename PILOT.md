# First musician pilot

Start with five musicians who are willing to try HornLab for a week. This is a small product test, not a study of whether the app improves playing.

Have each person create their own account, add one piece and one passage, and use it in an actual practice session. Watch the first attempt if possible. Note where they hesitate without guiding every click.

Ask afterward:

- What were you trying to get done, and where did HornLab help or get in the way?
- Did the tempo progression or listening comparison change what you did next?
- What made you come back, or what kept you from coming back?
- Which device and browser did you use? Did recording and playback work?

Record bugs and requested changes without copying private practice notes or recordings. Fix the problem that affected their practice most, then ask them to try that flow again.

At the end, count actual participants, people who saved sessions on at least two separate days, and the changes made because of feedback. Count a trial honestly even when someone does not return. Do not claim better musical performance from self-reported tempo results alone.

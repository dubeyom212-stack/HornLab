WHY I MADE THIS

I got tired of
figuring out
what to practice.

I'm a French horn player, and I've had plenty of days where I have 20 minutes to practice and way too many things I could be working on.

THE IDEA

There is always something to practice. A piece coming up. An audition. Scales. That one section that still isn't working.

The problem isn't always knowing how to practice. Sometimes the problem is deciding what deserves your time in the first place.

HornLab started with a pretty simple question:

“I have 25 minutes. What should I do?”

So I started building something that could answer it — without getting in the way of actually playing.

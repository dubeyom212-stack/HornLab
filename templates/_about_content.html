HI, I’M OM DUBEY

I needed a plan, too.

Between AP classes, music, and other commitments, finding time to practice was stressful enough. Figuring out what to do with that time made it harder.

I felt like a lot of musicians were dealing with the same thing: wanting to improve, but not having much structure when they sat down to practice. I wanted something that would help me make a plan and feel motivated to follow it.

That’s why I started HornLab. Pick how much time you have, keep track of the passages that need work, and leave yourself a note for next time.

I’m not in band anymore, but this is still a problem I care about. HornLab is my way of working on a solution.

CHANGED AFTER FEEDBACK

Less setup before playing.

Five of my French horn friends tried the live site. They liked the idea, but entering their repertoire took too long. I shortened entry to a piece name, added a way to paste a list, and put a Practice button beside each piece.

Next, I'll have them try this version and see what still slows them down.

Where your practice notes live

Your account has its own practice profiles. Other musicians signed in to this HornLab site cannot open or change your pieces and practice history. The person running the site manages its database and backups.

Recordings and unfinished practice stay in this browser. They are not synced between devices or encrypted on a shared computer. Use a browser profile you trust and download takes you want to keep. Dates and weekly totals follow the computer running HornLab.
